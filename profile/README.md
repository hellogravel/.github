# Hello Gravel

We sell rocks on the internet! Gravel, sand, soil and stone, delivered by dump truck in all 50 states.

We're a small team in New Orleans, and we build most of our own software. AI is a big part of how we do it: everyone on the team, technical or not, works with AI agents to ship code, run operations and sell.

## What we build

- **RockOS**, our platform that runs orders from checkout to delivery
- AI agents that handle sales, operations and support alongside the team
- Pricing, sourcing and delivery logistics for bulk aggregate

## AI Friday

We host [AI Friday](https://aifri.day), a monthly AI meetup in New Orleans. It has a daily AI news brief, meeting recaps, and the occasional experiment like [Woodchuck](https://github.com/AIonFriday/woodchuck), a prompt-compliance benchmark across frontier models.
